A NOTE ON STORIES

For the feelings
that stay.

Stories can hold a thousand little things: a glance, a memory, a moment that arrived at the wrong time. This space keeps those moments close, with the artwork at the heart of the experience.

Where Bengil gets
her ideas

After experiencing heartbreak, I started writing stories about us because it was the only way I could revisit the happy moments we shared. Through writing, I could go back to the moments that once made me happy, even if they were already part of the past. As time went by, I found myself enjoying writing even more, especially whenever I imagined different scenarios in my mind. Instead of waiting for another guy to come into my life and treat me the way I wanted to be treated, I started turning those imaginations into stories.

Maybe it's because I already know that even the happiest moments in real life can eventually fade, and sometimes, the people who once made us happy can become strangers. That's why I choose to create my own little world through writing—a world where I can decide how the story goes and how it ends. Because sometimes, fiction feels more beautiful than reality, not because it's more real, but because it can give us the kind of happiness that real life can't always promise.
